QUESTIONS WORTH RESOLVING EARLY

Hospitality

Record upholstery performance needs, cleaning routines, room types and replacement considerations. Ask for documentation against the selected specification.

Retail & dealerships

Separate display samples from stock quantities. Identify your intended assortment, material choices and any repeat-supply requirements.

Multi-location delivery

Split quantities by site and phase. Confirm access, packing labels and who is responsible for receiving, assembly and inspection at each location.

FROM IDEA TO INSTALLATION

A considered path
from first conversation.

Use these milestones to structure your project.
Scope and timing are agreed in your quotation.

  1. 01

    Define the vision

    Share plans, references and the way the space will be used. Establish the quantities, priorities and target installation date.

  2. 02

    Resolve the details

    Review dimensions, materials and finish samples. Confirm technical requirements and the approval process.

  3. 03

    Agree the specification

    Document the selected design, commercial terms, inspection arrangements and delivery responsibilities.

  4. 04

    Plan the handover

    Check access, installation requirements and care information so the furniture arrives with a clear plan.
