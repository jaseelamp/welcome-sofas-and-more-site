THE TRADE RESOURCE

Better specifications.
Clearer orders.

Practical notes for retail buyers and project teams:
dimensions, materials and a complete purchase brief.

01

RANGE & CONFIGURATION

Plan the assortment.
Define each model.

Define the mix of sofas your retail floor or project needs. Compare configurations, dimensions and upholstery options before requesting model-wise pricing.

Read the specification notes +

Build a model-wise schedule

List each proposed model, size, configuration and quantity. Separate display samples from sale stock and identify any repeated layouts across rooms or locations.

Agree the comfort reference

Record the desired seat depth, cushion feel and back support. Where samples are agreed, use them as an approval reference; dimensions alone cannot describe comfort.

Plan packing and delivery access

Record door openings, stairs, lifts and tight turns. Ask for packaged dimensions and whether the design can be delivered in separate sections before placing an order.

02

MATERIAL & MOOD

Texture changes
the conversation.

The same form can feel quiet, formal or expressive depending on its upholstery. Consider colour, weave and touch as one decision.

Read the material notes +

Bring samples into the space

View fabrics beside the floor, wall finish and curtains. Look at them in daylight and evening light; a colour on a screen is only a starting point.

Match the specification to use

For busy spaces, ask for the chosen fabric’s abrasion, colourfastness, cleaning and fire-performance information. Confirm the actual specification rather than assuming performance from appearance.

Make care part of the brief

Ask for written care instructions for the selected fabric or leather. Confirm approved cleaning methods and removable-cover options before committing.

03

TRADE & PROJECTS

A better brief.
A clearer quotation.

Give your supplier a shared picture of the project, from the first sample to the final installation. Clarity starts before production.

Read the project notes +

Define the scope

List quantities by room or area, proposed dimensions, upholstery references and delivery locations. Separate essential requirements from preferences that can evolve.

Agree approval milestones

Discuss drawings, finish samples and any prototype review. Record who approves each stage and what must be signed off before production begins.

Compare the complete quotation

Ask what is included in packing, freight, installation, taxes and aftercare. Confirm lead times, payment terms and warranty conditions in writing.
