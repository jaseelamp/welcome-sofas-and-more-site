01 / WHAT WE ARE HERE TO DO

Comfort for people.
Possibility for business.

A sofa has two lives: the one imagined in its design, and the one it becomes part of in a home, showroom or shared space. Our focus sits between the two—sofa manufacturing shaped by the needs of the people selecting it.

For dealers and retailers, that starts with an assortment suited to their customers. For interior and project buyers, it starts with the space, its use and the seating required. Welcome Sofas and More brings these requirements into a wholesale and manufacturing discussion, from design direction to an agreed order brief.

Our aim is simple: make the details easier to discuss, so every enquiry starts with a clearer understanding of the intended result.

02 / WHAT GUIDES THE CONVERSATION

Considered from
the inside out.

Four priorities for a thoughtful sofa brief.

01 / CRAFT

The details within.

Frame construction, support, upholstery tension and finishing all deserve attention. Explore the details beneath the surface as carefully as the silhouette.

02 / COMFORT

How it feels.

Seat depth, cushion feel and back support shape the experience. Define the comfort you want alongside the appearance you have in mind.

03 / CHARACTER

A form that belongs.

Proportion, colour and texture help a sofa find its place. Consider the intended setting and the people who will use it when choosing a direction.

04 / CLARITY

A shared understanding.

Bring quantities, dimensions, finishes and delivery requirements together. Use the agreed specification as the reference for the next steps.
