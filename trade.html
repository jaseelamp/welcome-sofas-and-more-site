FROM IDEA TO INSTALLATION

A considered path
from first conversation.

Use these milestones to structure your project.
Scope and timing are agreed in your quotation.

  1. 01

    Define the vision

    Share plans, references and the way the space will be used. Establish the quantities, priorities and target installation date.

  2. 02

    Resolve the details

    Review dimensions, materials and finish samples. Confirm technical requirements and the approval process.

  3. 03

    Agree the specification

    Document the selected design, commercial terms, inspection arrangements and delivery responsibilities.

  4. 04

    Plan the handover

    Check access, installation requirements and care information so the furniture arrives with a clear plan.

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Wholesale, with
the details in view.

Can I request a mixed-model order? +

Build a mixed schedule using the product explorer. The team must confirm model availability, quantities and any minimum-order requirements in the quotation.

How do sample approvals work? +

Discuss whether your order needs material samples, drawings or a prototype. Record the approval reference, changes and any related charges before manufacture.

Can delivery be organised in stages? +

Use delivery stages in your bulk schedule to express your preference. Timing, packing, freight and responsibility at each destination must be confirmed.

What should I send for a repeat order? +

Include the original order reference, specification revision, quantities and any changes. Confirm material availability and current commercial terms before proceeding.
